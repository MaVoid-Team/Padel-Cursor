Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to bring my own equipment?

No! We provide rackets and balls for all players. You're welcome to bring your own equipment if you prefer.

What should I wear?

Comfortable athletic clothing and non-marking court shoes. We recommend tennis or indoor court shoes for the best grip.

Can I book a court without a membership?

Yes! Non-members can book courts at our regular rates. Members get discounted rates and priority booking.

Do you offer lessons for beginners?

Absolutely! We have certified instructors who offer private and group lessons for all skill levels.

Is there parking available?

Yes, we have free parking available for all members and guests at our Westbard Square location.

What's your cancellation policy?

You can cancel or reschedule your booking up to 24 hours in advance at no charge. Late cancellations may incur a fee.
