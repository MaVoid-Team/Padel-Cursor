Facility Rules

General Rules

  • All players must check in at the front desk before playing
  • Proper athletic attire and non-marking court shoes are required
  • No food or drinks (except water) are allowed on the courts
  • Smoking and alcohol consumption are prohibited on the premises
  • Respect other players and maintain appropriate noise levels

Booking and Cancellation Policy

  • Court bookings can be made up to 30 days in advance
  • Members can book up to 7 days in advance, non-members up to 3 days
  • Cancellations must be made at least 24 hours in advance
  • Late cancellations or no-shows may result in charges
  • Court time cannot be extended beyond the booked period

Safety Guidelines

  • Warm up before playing to prevent injuries
  • Report any equipment issues or safety concerns immediately
  • Children under 16 must be supervised by an adult
  • Follow all posted safety instructions and guidelines
  • Use equipment responsibly and report any damage

Code of Conduct

  • Treat all players, staff, and visitors with respect
  • Use appropriate language and maintain a positive attitude
  • Follow the rules of padel and good sportsmanship
  • Resolve disputes through proper channels
  • Help maintain a clean and welcoming environment

Membership Terms

Membership Benefits

Membership benefits are subject to the terms of your specific membership plan and may include:

  • Discounted court booking rates
  • Priority booking privileges
  • Access to member-only events
  • Pro shop discounts

Billing and Payments

  • Membership fees are billed monthly in advance
  • Payment is due on the same date each month
  • Failed payments may result in membership suspension
  • Refunds are not provided for unused portions of the month
  • Membership rates are subject to change with 30 days notice

Membership Changes and Cancellation

  • Membership changes can be made at any time
  • Changes take effect at the beginning of the next billing cycle
  • Cancellation requires 30 days written notice
  • Membership benefits continue until the end of the current billing period
  • Reactivation may be subject to current rates and availability
