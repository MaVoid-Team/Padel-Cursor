Frequently Asked Questions

Can I change my plan later?

Yes! You can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time. Changes take effect at the beginning of your next billing cycle.

What happens if I don't use all my bookings?

Unused bookings don't roll over to the next month, but you can always book additional sessions at our regular rates.

Are there any initiation fees?

No initiation fees! We believe in keeping padel accessible to everyone. Just pay your monthly membership fee.

Can I bring guests?

Elite members get 4 guest passes per month. Other members can bring guests at our regular guest rates.
