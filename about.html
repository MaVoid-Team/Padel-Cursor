About Padel Social

Discover the story behind Bethesda's premier padel destination and meet the team that makes it all possible.

Our Story

Padel Social was born from a simple vision: to bring the joy and excitement of padel to the Bethesda community. Founded in 2023, we recognized the growing demand for this incredible sport that combines the best elements of tennis, squash, and racquetball.

What started as a passion project has evolved into Bethesda's premier padel destination. Our state-of-the-art facility at Westbard Square features three outdoor courts with professional lighting, a fully stocked pro shop, and plenty of space for players to relax and connect after their games.

We believe padel is more than just a sport – it's a way to build community, stay active, and have fun. Whether you're a complete beginner or an experienced player, our welcoming environment and expert coaching staff ensure that everyone can enjoy the game at their own pace.

Our Mission

To create an inclusive, welcoming community where people of all ages and skill levels can discover, learn, and fall in love with padel. We're committed to providing exceptional facilities, expert instruction, and memorable experiences that keep our members coming back for more.

Our Values

Community First

We believe in building lasting relationships and creating a supportive environment where everyone feels welcome.

Excellence

We maintain the highest standards in our facilities, coaching, and customer service to ensure an exceptional experience.

Accessibility

Padel should be accessible to everyone, regardless of age, skill level, or background. We make it easy to get started.

Fun

At the heart of everything we do is the belief that sports should be enjoyable, social, and bring people together.

What's Next

We're excited to announce that construction has begun on our permanent indoor facility, just around the corner from our current location. This new 15,000 square foot facility will feature:

  • Six indoor padel courts with climate control
  • Expanded pro shop and retail space
  • Full-service café and bar
  • Locker rooms and shower facilities
  • Fitness center and stretching area
  • Event space for tournaments and social gatherings

We're targeting a grand opening in late 2026. Stay tuned for updates and be among the first to experience our new world-class facility!
